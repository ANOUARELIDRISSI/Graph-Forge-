\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\begingroup\setstretch{1.08}
This report presents a Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG) platform for Moroccan business intelligence. The corpus aggregates approximately 78\,000 documents of different types, including HTML pages, PDF reports, RSS/Atom entries and structured data files published by HCP, AMMC and Bank Al-Maghrib, and it is collected by conservative, provenance-aware Scrapy crawlers that obey robots.txt. Documents are stored in a raw zone backed by WebHDFS when a cluster is available, cleaned and deduplicated by a Spark ETL job with a pure-Python equivalent, then split into overlapping 800/120-character chunks. Mistral extracts typed entities and relations into a Neo4j knowledge graph with durable caching, retry and provenance tagging, while NVIDIA \texttt{nemotron-3-embed-1b} embeddings of 2048 dimensions index the chunks in Qdrant and BM25 lexical scoring complements the semantic ranking. A FastAPI service exposes graph analytics, source exploration and grounded conversational answers to a React frontend. Evaluation combines retrieval metrics --- Recall@5, MRR and nDCG@5 for vector and GraphRAG strategies --- with a Ragas judge that measures faithfulness, answer relevancy and context precision; the completed run reports a faithfulness of 0.94, an answer relevancy of 0.92 and a context precision of 0.89, and the report keeps smoke diagnostics clearly separated from a reviewed benchmark.
